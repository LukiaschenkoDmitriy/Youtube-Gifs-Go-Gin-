What is this extension

Youtube GIFs is a browser extension that integrates directly into the YouTube interface. It lets you find and insert GIFs into the comment field, and exchange GIFs in real time while watching videos.

GIFs in comments

Drop animations into comments in one click — no third-party sites needed.

Live GIF chat

Exchange GIFs with other viewers in real time.

Fast search

Browse the GIF library right inside the player.

Secure sign-in

Google OAuth2 authorization — no passwords stored.

Terms of use

  • The extension is intended solely for adding and sharing GIFs within the YouTube platform.
  • Authorization is handled via Google OAuth2; we do not store or access your password.
  • We process only the data required for the service to work (session identifier and basic profile).
  • Using the extension to distribute offensive, illegal, or spam content is prohibited.
  • The service is provided "as is", without uptime guarantees; functionality may change.
  • You can revoke access and remove the extension from your browser at any time.

By using the extension, you agree to the terms above.

Frequently asked questions

Is the extension free?
Yes, the core functionality is completely free. You can support development with a voluntary donation in the block below.
What data do you collect?
Only the minimum needed for authorization and chat: your Google identifier, basic profile, and a session token. Passwords are never stored.
Does it work in other browsers?
Chromium-based browsers (Chrome, Edge, Brave) are currently supported. Support for other browsers is planned.
How do I delete my account or data?
Revoke the app's access in your Google account settings and remove the extension. Related session data is deleted automatically.
Found a bug — where do I report it?
Reach out to the developer via the contacts in the support block below — we respond to bug reports promptly.
